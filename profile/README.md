# Significant Dust

A global field of shared meaning, revealed by the voices of those who choose to speak.

## Guiding Principles

### Noise becomes signal

Every voice is small.
In isolation, we’re just screaming into the void.
Together, our voices carry.

This is a live, global field of shared meaning.
Not a feed. Not a poll. Not a platform.

You add your voice to see what’s forming — here, now, in this place.

Signals rise, spread, and fade over time.
What matters today is visible today.

You are significant.

### The ritual

There are no spectators.

You don’t open an app and scroll.
You speak — _once_ — then you're able to see what others are saying.

One voice per day.
No profiles. No followers. No score.

Participation is the key that reveals the signal.

### What you see

You don’t see people.
You don’t see posts.

_You see shared meaning._

Words forming and dissolving across place and time.
A living civic weather system.

Zoom out → broad themes.
Zoom in → local concerns.
Change the window → watch moments pass.

Signals only appear where enough voices overlap.
Silence remains silent.

### Privacy by design

Locations are grouped into broad regions.
Exact positions are never shown.

Areas only appear when enough people have contributed.
Individual voices cannot be isolated.

This is not a scientific poll.
It is a real-time view of what participants choose to express.

### How it works (without breaking the spell)

Each voice becomes a small, anonymous event.

Events overlap in space (H3 cells) and time (short windows).
Unique voices — not volume — create strength.

From that, signals emerge.

The map is a projection of those signals,
not a collection of messages.

Memory is durable.
Perception resets each day.

### Global by default

_Architected for the world._

- Edge participation → local latency everywhere
- Append-only event memory → replayable history
- Spatial binning → natural sharding
- Time windows → bounded computation
- Materialized signals → fast reads at planetary scale

No social graph.
No timelines.
No feeds.

Just overlapping voices.

### Polls without polling

A QR code can anchor a question to a moment and a place.

Scan → speak → see the signal for that question.

Multiple choice or freeform.
Local or global.
Event or ongoing.

Organizations don’t buy attention.
They buy a way to listen — in real time — to aggregated, anonymous signal.

No individual data.
Only shared emergence.

### What this is not

Not social media.
Not a survey tool.
Not sentiment analytics.
Not advertising.

This does not measure people.
It reveals patterns of expression.

### What this becomes

A daily civic weather map.
A living field of shared concerns.
A way to feel less alone in what matters.

A place where...

- Noise becomes signal
- Small voices carry
- Meaning appears

**You are significant.**
